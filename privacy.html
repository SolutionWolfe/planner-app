Planner privacy policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

What Planner is. Planner is a private, single-user workout planning app run by its operator for personal use. It is not offered to the public, and there is no way to sign up. This page exists because Planner connects to an Oura account and explains how that data is handled. This page explains what it collects, what it does with it, how long it keeps it, and how you can see, change or delete it.

What is collected from your Oura account. When you connect your Oura account, Planner reads, with your permission, the following through Oura's official interface: daily readiness, sleep and activity summaries; sleep periods; heart-rate readings; workouts and sessions; tags you add in the Oura app; daily blood-oxygen and stress summaries; ring configuration; and heart-health estimates. You can see the exact list on Oura's permission screen when you connect, and you can disconnect at any time.

What Planner records itself. Your answers to the daily check-in (when your day starts, how you feel, anything unusual you note), the plan it makes for you, what you mark as started, done, skipped or changed, and your notes and reasons for changes.

What it is used for. To build each day's plan, to compare today with your own recent history, to show you your past days, and to let you look at trends in your own data over time. Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Who else sees it. No one. Planner stores your data with a cloud database provider under the operator's account, encrypted in transit and at rest. During the current transition, an artificial-intelligence service receives the screenshot and check-in answers you provide each morning, to produce the day's plan; it receives nothing read from your Oura account, and nothing is kept by that service. This will end, and this sentence will be removed, when the transition completes. The operator does not share data with third parties, and the app does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

How long it is kept. For as long as your Oura account is connected to Planner, so that your history and trends stay complete. Raw responses from Oura are not stored; only the individual values are.

Disconnecting and deleting. You can pause Planner's access at any time (Settings, Pause Oura) and nothing more is read until you resume. You can disconnect (Settings, Disconnect Oura, or by removing Planner in the Oura app). When you disconnect, Planner stops reading your Oura data immediately and the operator deletes the Oura-sourced data within 72 hours. Your own check-in and workout records are yours and stay unless you ask for them to be deleted too.

Your rights. You can see, correct or delete the data Planner holds about you at any time and without any penalty. Planner has one account, the operator's, and the operator reaches that data directly in the app and in the database behind it, so no request has to be made to anyone. If you are reading this and are not the operator, contact the person who gave you access to the app; they will act on a request within 30 days.

Who is responsible. The operator of Planner is the independent controller of the data described here and is not acting on behalf of Oura. Oura's own handling of your data is described in Oura's privacy policy. Planner is not affiliated with or endorsed by Oura.

Changes. If this policy changes, the date above changes and the new version is shown here.
